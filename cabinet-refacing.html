What Is Cabinet Refacing?

A New Kitchen Look — Without the New Kitchen Price Tag

Cabinet refacing replaces your doors, drawer fronts, and matching veneer while keeping the existing cabinet structures in place. Subtle or dramatic, rustic or modern — the look is entirely up to you.

Door and drawer fronts wear out faster than the frames behind them. Refacing gives you the option of changing your look without cleaning out your kitchen — or your wallet. The familiar layout stays. Everything else gets a complete refresh.

FAQs

Cabinet Refacing Questions, Answered

Is cabinet refacing right for me?
If your cabinet boxes are structurally solid but the doors and drawer fronts look dated, refacing is almost always the smarter call. Door and drawer fronts wear faster than frames — so it's rarely necessary to replace the whole structure. Refacing gives you the new look without the new-kitchen cost.
Is cabinet refacing expensive?
Cabinet refacing typically costs a fraction of a full replacement. With over 30 years in the industry, we get volume pricing from our manufacturers and pass those savings on to you — so you get quality cabinet doors and refacing at affordable pricing.
How long does refacing take?
Most kitchen cabinet refacing projects complete in just 2–4 days. Our technicians work cleanly, leave the kitchen tidy at the end of each day, and your plumbing and most of your cooking area stay in service throughout.
Will there be a big mess?
No. Our technicians pride themselves on keeping a clean workplace and clean the kitchen area at the end of every workday. There's far less dust and demo than a full cabinet replacement — and you'll have full use of your home throughout.
Are there limited cabinet choices?
Not at all. We offer a great selection of wood species, finishes, door profiles, and colors. With 30 years of design experience, we'll help you find the exact look you're after — whether that's classic shaker, modern slab, or anything in between.
Can I add cabinets if I need more storage?
Yes. Refacing is the perfect time to make use of wasted space. We can install additional cabinets, modify existing ones, add drawers, and build islands — all matched to the new look of your refaced kitchen.
Are there environmental benefits?
Yes. Refacing keeps your cabinet boxes out of the landfill and conserves the materials and energy that would have gone into building entirely new ones. Your kitchen looks completely new — and the project is eco-friendly.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. Flexible financing is available so you don't have to compromise on the door style or wood species you really want. Get pre-approved in minutes through our financing page.
