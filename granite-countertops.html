Material Guide

Granite vs Quartz — Which Is Right for You?

Both are exceptional choices. Here's a quick side-by-side to help you decide.

Natural Stone

Granite Countertops

  • 100% natural stone — every slab is unique
  • Outstanding heat resistance — hot pans safe
  • Extremely hard surface, highly scratch resistant
  • Wide variety of organic veining and patterns
  • Periodic sealing keeps it stain-resistant
  • Adds organic warmth and timeless character
Engineered Stone

Quartz Countertops

  • Engineered for uniform color and consistency
  • Non-porous — no sealing required, ever
  • Highly stain resistant against wine, coffee, oil
  • Available in modern colors and finishes
  • Low-maintenance — wipe with soap and water
  • Ideal for contemporary kitchen and bath designs

FAQs

Countertop Questions, Answered

How much do granite countertops cost in Houston?
Granite countertop pricing typically runs $40–$100+ per square foot installed, depending on the grade of stone, edge profile, and project complexity. We carry budget-friendly options as well as high-end designer slabs, and we provide detailed transparent estimates for your specific kitchen or bathroom.
Is granite or quartz better?
Both are excellent. Granite is 100% natural stone with unique veining and exceptional heat resistance — ideal if you love organic, one-of-a-kind looks. Quartz is engineered for uniformity, requires no sealing, and offers a more modern feel. We'll help you decide based on your style, lifestyle, and budget.
Do granite countertops need to be sealed?
Yes — granite is a natural porous stone and should be sealed at installation, then re-sealed every 1–3 years depending on usage. Sealing is quick and easy, and keeps your countertops stain-resistant for the long run. Quartz, by contrast, never needs sealing.
How long does installation take?
After templating, fabrication typically takes 1–2 weeks. Installation day itself takes a few hours to a full day, depending on kitchen size and complexity. From the day you sign off on your slab, most projects complete within 2–3 weeks.
Can I see the actual slab before it's cut?
Absolutely. We strongly encourage it — every granite slab is unique. Visit our 4,000 sq ft showroom or tour our granite yard with our team to see and approve your exact slab under real lighting before fabrication begins.
Will granite crack or chip?
Granite is one of the hardest natural stones available and is extremely durable. With proper installation and reasonable care, cracks and chips are very rare — typically only occurring from severe impact directly on an edge. Day-to-day kitchen use will not damage it.
Do you handle removal of my old countertops?
Yes. Our team handles removal and disposal of your existing countertops, sink disconnect, and reinstallation of plumbing fixtures. We leave your space clean and ready to use.
Do you offer financing for countertop projects?
Yes — we offer flexible financing options so you don't have to compromise on the slab you want. Get pre-approved in minutes through our financing page, with terms designed for kitchen and bath upgrades.
