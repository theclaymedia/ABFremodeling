FAQs

Tile Installation Questions, Answered

What's the difference between ceramic and porcelain tile?
Both are made from clay, but porcelain is fired at higher temperatures, making it denser, harder, and more water-resistant. Ceramic is more budget-friendly and easier to cut; porcelain is the better choice for high-traffic floors and wet areas like showers.
How long does tile installation take?
A typical kitchen backsplash takes 1–3 days. A full bathroom can take 3–7 days. Larger floor installations and natural stone projects may take 1–2 weeks depending on size, prep work, and material. We'll provide a clear timeline at consultation.
What tile is best for Houston's climate?
Porcelain and ceramic tile are excellent for Houston's humidity — they don't absorb moisture or warp. Natural stones like travertine and marble look stunning but require sealing to handle humidity. For outdoor patios, weather-rated porcelain is the way to go.
Do I need to seal my tile?
Ceramic and porcelain are non-porous and don't require sealing — only the grout lines do. Natural stones (marble, travertine, limestone, saltillo) are porous and need to be sealed at install and periodically afterward to resist stains. We seal every natural stone install before handover.
Can you install tile over existing flooring?
Sometimes — it depends on the existing surface, levelness, and structural support. In many cases removal of old flooring gives a better, longer-lasting result. We'll evaluate your specific situation during consultation and recommend the right approach.
Do you do repair work too?
Yes — we repair cracked tiles, regrout, refinish, polish, and seal existing installations. If you have a tile floor or backsplash that just needs a refresh rather than a full replacement, we can help with that too.
What areas do you serve?
